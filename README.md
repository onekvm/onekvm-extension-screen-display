# OneKVM screen display settings

English | [简体中文](README.zh-CN.md)

Use this extension to change what appears on your OneKVM device's small screen.
Sign in to OneKVM and open **Screen display settings** to arrange the home
screen, adjust brightness or choose which apps appear in the launcher on
supported devices.

`onekvm-distro` builds and installs the `onekvm-extension-screen-display`
package. See the [widgets and apps guide](docs/widgets.md) for adding items to
the home screen or launcher.
